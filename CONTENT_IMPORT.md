# Content and source policy

The platform stores each question with `source`, `source_round`, `question_number` and `source_reference`. Historical questions in this build come from the user-provided historical compilation. The official ACCA past-exams page is stored as the reference URL.

For current ACCA materials, use the material under the permissions that apply to your student/classroom use. Do not publish or sell ACCA resources without permission.

The current DipIFR D25-J26 syllabus includes IFRS 18 and IFRS 19; the app catalog covers those areas as well as the core standards and group-reporting topics identified by the study guide.
